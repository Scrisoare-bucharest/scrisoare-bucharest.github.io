Who's writing to you?

Hi there, lovely to have you here! I'm Rosie :)

I grew up in a small village in Romania and came to Germany with my parents in the mid-1990s. Then came school, university (partly abroad), work at international companies and many projects abroad.

And now? Quite unexpectedly, my husband, our two little ones (2 and 4) and I are off to Bucharest! We'll get to know the country all over again, and I'm sure there's so much to discover. I'll tell you all about it. On paper. Every month.

FAQ

Where do you ship?

Worldwide. The letters travel with the Romanian post and take a little longer outside Europe.

When does the first letter arrive?

The first letter goes out in the first week of January 2027. After that, I post every month by the 5th.

Can I cancel?

The monthly plan anytime, effective at the end of the month. Prepaid plans simply end, with no renewal.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Choose 3, 6 or 12 months and enter the recipient's address at checkout. Email me if you'd like a note from you in the first letter.

What if a letter gets lost?

Email me and I'll send it again.

Will the children be shown?

No. At most, something they painted.
